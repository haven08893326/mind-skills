# Mind Skills / 心智学技能

A bilingual, non-clinical self-reflection toolkit for Chinese and English users. It covers emotional maturity, relationships, critical thinking, parenting, adolescent behavior, and workplace culture.

## Features

- Emotional maturity and self-awareness assessment
- Relationship patterns, attachment, approval seeking, and boundaries
- Independent thinking and resistance to online influence
- Parenting and adolescent phone overuse support
- Structured mind-body-consciousness intervention framework
- Workplace culture and organizational maturity reflection
- Chinese framework and original assessment materials preserved

## Example prompts

English:

- “Assess my emotional maturity.”
- “Why do I keep seeking approval?”
- “Is this love or emotional dependency?”
- “How do I set boundaries without feeling guilty?”
- “How can I stop being influenced by online opinions?”
- “My teenager is addicted to their phone. What should I do?”
- “Why does my team resist every change?”

中文：

- “测一下我的心智成熟度。”
- “为什么我总想被认可？”
- “这到底是喜欢还是爱？”
- “怎么设立边界又不感到内疚？”
- “怎么不被新闻和观点带节奏？”
- “孩子沉迷手机怎么办？”
- “为什么团队总是推不动？”

## Installation

Download `mind-skills.zip`, extract it into your Codex skills directory, and restart or refresh Codex. The package includes a bilingual router and the original Chinese modules and question banks.

## Language behavior

The skill replies in the user's language by default. Chinese concepts such as 心智, 贪妄, 共振, and 原生家庭 are preserved and explained in English when English is being used.

## Safety

This is an educational and self-reflection toolkit, not a clinical diagnostic service. It does not replace therapy, medical care, legal advice, or emergency support. For self-harm, suicidal thoughts, violence, psychosis, or other acute danger, seek qualified local professional or emergency help.
